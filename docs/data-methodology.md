# Data Methodology — Adaptive Anti-Doping Defense Engine

This document describes the synthetic dataset underpinning the system. All
numbers are derived directly from the current `data/` files and the
generator in `ml/generate_data.py` (v1.2, seed 42).

---

| Dimension | Value |
|---|---|
| Athletes | **80** |
| Sports | **5** |
| Total samples | **400** |

## Biomarkers

Five biomarkers are tracked per sample:

| Field | Description | Unit | Observed range |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hb` | Haemoglobin concentration | g/dL | 13.61 – 16.72 |
| `hct` | Haematocrit | % | 39.41 – 50.30 |
| `ret_pct` | Reticulocyte percentage | % | 0.50 – 2.02 |
| `off_score` | Athlete Biological Passport OFF-score (server-derived) | score | 62.4 – 116.8 |
| `te_ratio` | Testosterone-to-epitestosterone ratio | dimensionless | 0.67 – 1.53 |

`off_score` is never supplied by the client; it is always computed
server-side as `(hb_g_dL × 10) − 60 × √(ret_pct)`.

### Contextual flags

Each sample also carries three boolean flags:

| Field | Description | Current value in dataset |
|---|---|---|
| `competition_flag` | Sample taken near a competition | All `false` (not yet varied) |
| `altitude_flag` | Athlete at high altitude | All `false` (not yet varied) |
| `injury_flag` | Active injury / TUE | All `false` (not yet varied) |

> **Note:** the flags are present in the schema and tracked by the
> backend; they are not yet varied in the synthetic dataset. Contextual
> flag variation is planned for a future data-generation pass.

## Generation Method

Samples are generated by a **first-order autoregressive (AR(1)) random
walk** anchored to per-sport population priors.

### Per-sport population priors

Each sport has a distinct physiological baseline. These priors seed the
Bayesian model for every athlete in that sport before any samples exist,
and also serve as the mean-reversion point for the random walk:

| Sport | hb mean (g/dL) | hct mean (%) | ret_pct mean (%) | te_ratio mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cycling | 15.4 | 45.0 | 1.05 | 1.15 |
| Rowing | 15.1 | 44.8 | 1.00 | 1.12 |
| Running | 14.8 | 43.5 | 1.12 | 1.10 |
| Swimming | 14.5 | 42.8 | 1.08 | 1.08 |
| Triathlon | 14.9 | 44.0 | 1.10 | 1.10 |

### AR(1) correlated walk

Each athlete begins at a personal baseline drawn from their sport's
prior (adding individual variation). Subsequent samples follow:

```
ε_t = ρ · ε_{t-1} + N(0, σ)
value_t = baseline + ε_t
```

where `ρ = 0.75` (autocorrelation) and the per-biomarker step
standard deviations are: hb=0.12 g/dL, hct=0.35%, ret_pct=0.04%,
te_ratio=0.03.

`hb` and `hct` are additionally coupled (coefficient 2.85): an hb
increment adds a proportional hct increment, reflecting their
physiological co-variation. All values are clamped to physiologically
plausible bounds after each step.

## Injected Anomaly Archetypes

**15 of 80 athletes (18.75%) carry an injected anomaly.** The three
archetypes are distributed equally at 5 athletes (6.25%) each.

### 1. Transfusion (5 athletes — 6.25%)

Simulates a blood transfusion event. Over a 2-sample window, the
generator injects:
- Hb spike: **+1.8 g/dL**
- Hct spike: **+5.0 %**
- Reticulocyte suppression: **−0.35 %**

The RET% drop following the Hb/Hct step-up is the key distinguishing
signature used by the Athlete Biological Passport programme.

### 2. EPO Micro-Dosing (5 athletes — 6.25%)

Simulates a gradual, sustained erythropoietin micro-dose. Over a
3-sample window the generator applies a cumulative drift:
- Hb rise: **+0.8 g/dL**
- Hct rise: **+2.0 %**
- Reticulocyte rise: **+0.20 %**
- te_ratio rise: **+0.05**

The signal is intentionally subtle to model evasion-aware micro-dosing.
See the known limitation in `docs/model-summary.md` for detection rates.

### 3. Steroid Micro-Dosing (5 athletes — 6.25%)

Simulates low-dose anabolic steroid use. Over a 2-sample window:
- te_ratio rise: **+0.30**
- Hb rise: **+0.15 g/dL**
- ret_pct drop: **−0.05 %**

The primary marker is the te_ratio drift; hb acts as a secondary
corroborating signal.

## Reproducibility

All data is generated deterministically with **`random.seed(42)`**.
Re-running `ml/generate_data.py` from the repo root will reproduce
`data/athletes.json`, `data/samples.json`, and `data/ground_truth.json`
exactly.

`data/ground_truth.json` is **internal evaluation use only** and is
never exposed via any API endpoint or frontend view. See
`docs/schema.md` and `docs/api-contract.md` for the formal constraint.
